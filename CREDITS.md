## Music and sound

- **"Opening Night"** (Curtain Call): written for this game and played by a small synthesizer in code (`src/audio/openingNight.ts`, `src/audio/synth.ts`). It uses no samples, recordings, or existing songs.
- **Metronome clicks and the crowd cheer**: also made in code (`src/audio/synth.ts`).

## Art

- All pixel art is placeholder art drawn in code (`src/art/`, `src/map/tileset.ts`), copied from the project mockups in `docs/mocks/`.

## Places

- The game's stops are real Great Barrington, MA businesses and places. Their names appear as plain text only (no logos), and all names live in `src/data/stops.ts`.
